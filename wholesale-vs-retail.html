The Real Cost

It's not just the per-bag price.

When you buy at a retail store, you're paying more per bag — but that's only part of the cost. Add the time to drive to the store, load the truck, haul it to the job site, and unload it. Add the truck cost or rental. Add the risk that the store is out of stock on the product you need. Add the fact that you paid upfront before seeing a single bag installed. Wholesale delivery eliminates all of those costs. The product comes to you, on the schedule you set, and you pay after you've confirmed the order is right.

01

No truck runs

Every trip to the store costs time and fuel. On a 40-bag order, that's a full truck load — often requiring multiple trips. Wholesale delivery puts those hours back on your job.

02

No stock surprises

Retail stores run out. We confirm stock before every order. If we don't have it, we tell you before you've committed — not when your driver gets there.

03

Volume pricing scales with you

The more you order, the better your per-bag pricing. Retail stores don't negotiate. We do — especially for repeat customers and large project orders.
