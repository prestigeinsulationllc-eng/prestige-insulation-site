Ordering Insulation Delivery — What to Expect

2026-06-15  ·  Prestige Insulation

Ready to order? ☎ Call (929) 466-1426 · Get a Same-Day Quote →

Ordering insulation delivery is simpler than most people expect. You don't need an account. Payment at the time of delivery. You don't need to fill out a multi-page form. Here is exactly what happens when you order from us.

Ready to order?

Call or text your product and quantity — we quote the same business day.

☎ Call (929) 466-1426

Step 1: You tell us what you need

Call or text (929) 466-1426 with: product type (fiberglass or mineral wool), R-value (e.g. R-21), quantity (bags or square footage), and your delivery address. That's it. You can also fill out the quote form on any page of this site.

Step 2: We quote you the same business day

Within a few hours (usually within the hour), we confirm availability, quote you a price, and give you a delivery window. No bait-and-switch. No hidden fees. The quote includes delivery.

Step 3: We deliver

Next-day delivery on stocked product in our 209-city market. For areas outside our next-day footprint, same-week delivery in most cases. We call or text you with an ETA the morning of delivery.

Step 4: You pay at delivery

No deposit. Payment at the time of delivery. You pay when the material arrives — after you've seen it, confirmed the quantities, and verified the product. This is standard in the wholesale insulation business and we think it's the right way to operate.

What the delivery looks like

Bags of insulation are stacked and delivered to your door, garage, or job site — wherever is most convenient. For large orders, we can coordinate a loading dock delivery or phased multi-date delivery for active job sites.
