How to Order Insulation for a Job Site

A step-by-step walkthrough for contractors who need insulation delivered — what to have ready, how the quote process works, and what happens on delivery day.

Step 1: Know What You Need Before You Call

The faster you can answer these four questions, the faster you get a quote:

  • R-value — What does the spec sheet or energy code require? (R-13, R-21, R-49, etc.)
  • Batt width — Are your studs or joists on 16" OC (use 15" wide batts) or 24" OC (use 23" wide batts)?
  • Quantity — Square footage of the area you're insulating, or number of bags if you already know
  • Zip code and delivery date — Where it's going and when you need it

If you don't know the quantity, use our free insulation calculator. Enter your square footage and R-value and it tells you exactly how many bags to order.

Step 2: Request a Quote

You have three options:

  • Call or text (929) 466-1426 — Fastest. Text your list and we respond with a firm quote, usually within the hour.
  • Online quote form — Fill out the form at /request-quote.html. We respond the same business day.
  • Email — Send your list to Info@PrestigeInsulationco.com with your product, quantity, zip, and target date.

Most contractors text us a list that looks like: "50 bags R-21 15", 30 bags R-38, zip 30301, need by Thursday". That's all we need.

Step 3: Confirm the Quote

We send you a quote showing product, quantity, and total. No deposits required. Just confirm you want to proceed — by phone, text, or reply email — and we lock in the delivery.

If your site isn't ready yet, tell us. We can hold the order and schedule delivery for a specific future date.

Step 4: Delivery Day

We'll confirm the delivery window in advance and give you updated ETAs the day before and morning of delivery.

What to do on delivery day:

  • Have someone on site or designate an authorized representative
  • Count the bags and check product labels against the invoice
  • If anything is wrong — wrong product, wrong count — call us before signing off
  • Once you verify everything is correct, pay the driver per the invoice total

Payment is at delivery. No deposit was taken, so the only payment happens after you've confirmed the order is right.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance do I need to order?

For stocked products, next-day delivery is available if you order before 3 PM EST. For large orders or less common products, give us 2–3 days. Call to confirm availability.

What if I need more material mid-project?

Call or text us and reference your original order. We'll prioritize a fill delivery. No need to start a new quote from scratch.

Can I order for multiple job sites at once?

Yes. Include each address and delivery date in your quote request and we'll coordinate separate deliveries for each.

What if the delivery has the wrong product?

Inspect before you pay. If anything doesn't match the invoice, contact us immediately. We resolve discrepancies before payment — that's the whole point of pay-at-delivery.
