How to Calculate Insulation Quantity: Bags Needed by R-Value and Square Footage

The formula is simple — here's how to count bags for walls, attics, and floors, with a quick reference table by house size.

The formula: square footage ÷ coverage per bag

Every bag of insulation lists its coverage in square feet at the specified R-value. To find your quantity: measure the area you're insulating (wall face area, attic floor area, etc.), divide by the coverage per bag, and add 10–15% for waste and cuts. That's your order quantity.

Step-by-step calculation example

Project: 2×6 exterior walls on a 2,000 sq ft house. Ordering R-21 HD. Assume coverage of 50 sq ft per bag.

  1. Calculate gross wall area: perimeter × ceiling height. 200 LF perimeter × 9 ft = 1,800 sq ft gross.
  2. Subtract openings: typical 20% for doors and windows = 1,800 × 0.80 = 1,440 sq ft net.
  3. Divide by coverage: 1,440 ÷ 50 = 29 bags.
  4. Add 10% waste: 29 × 1.10 = 32 bags to order.
