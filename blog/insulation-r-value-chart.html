Complete R-value chart for walls, attics, and floors

R-value measures thermal resistance — the higher the number, the better the insulation. The right R-value for your project depends on climate zone, assembly type, and framing depth. Here's the full chart.

Wall insulation R-value chart

FramingProductR-ValueZonesNotes
2×4Fiberglass standardR-131–3Code minimum zones 1–3
2×4Fiberglass HDR-154–8Required for zones 4+ under IECC 2021
2×6Fiberglass standardR-191–4Common in mild climates
2×6Fiberglass HDR-214–8Best performance in standard 2×6 cavity
2×4 or 2×6Mineral wool AFBR-15 or R-23AllFire-rated / high-STC assemblies
