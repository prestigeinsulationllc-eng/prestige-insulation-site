Overview

Hotel & Hospitality Insulation — Wholesale.

Hotels live and die by guest experience — and noise is the #1 complaint in hospitality reviews. The assemblies between guest rooms, between floors, and between corridors and rooms must be engineered for both fire safety and sound attenuation. We supply the mineral wool and high-density fiberglass that hospitality contractors specify.

01

Guest room party walls — STC 50+

Mineral wool AFB in party walls, combined with resilient channel and 5/8″ drywall, reliably achieves STC 50–55. That’s the difference between a 3-star and a 4-star review.

02

Corridor fire-rated walls

Hotel corridors are typically required to be 1-hour fire-rated under IBC. Mineral wool SAFB in the stud cavity is the standard product for corridor assemblies — non-combustible, UL-listed, and sound-attenuating simultaneously.

03

Exterior wall energy performance

Branded hotels have energy performance standards. Zone 4+ exterior walls require R-15 HD minimum; many brands require R-21 (2×6 framing).

FAQ

Common questions.

What STC rating do hotels require?

Most branded hotel franchises require STC 50 minimum for guest room separations. Mineral wool AFB in a standard wood-frame assembly with resilient channel achieves STC 50–55.

Is fire-rated insulation required in hotel corridors?

Yes. IBC requires 1-hour fire-rated corridor walls in hotels (R-2 occupancy). Mineral wool SAFB is non-combustible and used in UL-listed 1-hour assemblies.

Do you supply for both new hotel construction and renovation?

Yes. New construction needs larger volumes staged to your schedule. Renovation work can order any quantity, no minimum.
