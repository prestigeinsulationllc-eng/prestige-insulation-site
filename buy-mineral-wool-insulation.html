Why Mineral Wool

Fire resistance, sound control, moisture resistance.

Mineral wool is non-combustible and holds its shape and R-value even when exposed to moisture — unlike fiberglass, it doesn't wick or hold water. That makes it the standard choice for party walls, fire-rated assemblies, floor/ceiling sound control, and any application where fire code or moisture exposure is a factor.
