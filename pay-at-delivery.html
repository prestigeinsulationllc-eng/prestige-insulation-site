How it works

Three steps. Zero risk.

01

Get a quote

Call or text us with your R-value, quantity, zip code, and delivery date. We respond the same business day with stock confirmation and a firm invoice. No commitment yet — just a number.

02

We deliver to your site

Next business day on stocked product. We deliver to job sites, warehouses, and residential addresses across all 50 states. You don't pay a cent until the truck arrives.

03

Inspect, then pay

Count the bags, check the product, confirm everything matches the invoice. Once you're satisfied, pay at delivery. That's it — order complete.

Why we do this

We think you should see the product before you pay for it.

Most suppliers require a deposit or full prepayment before they ship anything. That means you're trusting them with your money before you've seen a single bag. We think that's backwards. Insulation is a commodity — the bags should prove their worth before the check clears. Pay at delivery keeps us accountable and keeps you protected. If something's wrong, it gets fixed before payment. That's how we'd want to be treated.

FAQ

Questions about pay at delivery.

Is there really no deposit at all?

Correct. No deposit, no partial payment. You pay after delivery and inspection — in full, once you've confirmed the order is right.

What if I need to cancel or change my order?

Call us as early as possible. Since you haven't paid anything yet, there's no financial dispute — just a conversation about logistics. We'll do our best to accommodate changes before the order ships.

How does payment work?

Payment is collected at delivery — after you verify the order is correct.

What if there's a discrepancy in the order?

That's exactly why we do pay at delivery. If bag counts don't match or there's a product issue, it gets resolved before you pay. Call us immediately on delivery and we'll sort it out.

Is pay at delivery available for all orders?

Yes — all orders, all sizes, all 50 states. Whether you're ordering 5 bags or 500 bags, pay at delivery is the standard.
