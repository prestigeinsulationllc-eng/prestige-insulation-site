Why R-19

The workhorse of 2×6 floor and wall insulation.

01

Floor cavity standard

R-19 is the most commonly specified insulation for floor cavities over unconditioned crawl spaces and basements. It fits the 2×10 and 2×8 framing typical in floor assemblies.

02

Meets code in moderate climates

In IECC climate zones 1–3, R-19 satisfies above-grade wall requirements. For zone 4+, contractors often upgrade to R-21 — but R-19 remains valid in many jurisdictions with other trade-offs.

03

Widely stocked and available

R-19 is one of our highest-volume products. We keep deep inventory so you can order confidently even on short notice — same-day quotes, next-day delivery on stocked product.

R-19 FAQ

Common questions about R-19.

R-19 vs R-21 — which should I use for walls?

Both fit in a 2×6 cavity. In climate zone 4+, R-21 is generally preferred because most codes require R-20+. In warmer zones, R-19 satisfies code requirements. See full comparison →

What's R-19 used for besides walls?

Floor cavities over unconditioned basements and crawl spaces. The 6.25" thickness also works in 2×10 floor joists with a small gap, which some inspectors prefer for air circulation.

Do you stock both 15" and 23" widths?

Yes. Specify the width when you call or submit your quote. 15" is for 16" OC framing; 23" is for 24" OC.
