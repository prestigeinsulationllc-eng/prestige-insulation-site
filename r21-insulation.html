Why R-21

The standard for 2×6 exterior walls.

01

Code-compliant in climate zones 4–8

Most energy codes in colder climates require R-20 or higher for above-grade walls. R-21 satisfies this requirement and is the go-to spec for compliant 2×6 construction.

02

High-density — stays in place

R-21 is a high-density batt. It's thinner than R-19 (5.5" vs 6.25") but denser, so it holds its shape in tall wall cavities and doesn't sag between inspections and drywall.

03

Better performance than R-19

The 10% improvement in thermal resistance adds up over the life of the building. If you're already using 2×6 framing, upgrading from R-19 to R-21 costs very little per job.

R-21 FAQ

Common questions about R-21.

What's the difference between R-19 and R-21?

Both fit in a 2×6 cavity. R-21 is high-density — thinner but denser, with ~10% better thermal performance. Most climate zone 4+ codes now require at least R-20, making R-21 the standard choice. Full comparison →

Do you carry 15" and 23" width?

Yes. 15" is for 16" on-center framing; 23" is for 24" on-center. Specify when you call or submit a quote so we pull the right product.

Is there a minimum order?

No minimum. You can order 5 bags or 500 bags. Volume typically earns better per-bag pricing — ask about it when you call.

How fast can you deliver?

Next business day on stocked product when you order before 3pm EST. We deliver to all 50 states. Rush orders can sometimes be arranged — call to discuss.
