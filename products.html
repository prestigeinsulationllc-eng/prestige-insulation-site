Which Is Right for You?

Fiberglass vs. Mineral Wool

Both are excellent insulators. The right choice depends on your project's priorities. Here's how they compare across the factors that matter most on the jobsite.

Feature Fiberglass Mineral Wool
Thermal Performance ✓ R-11 to R-49 ✓ R-15 to R-30
Fire Resistance Melts above ~1,000°F Superior — withstands up to 2,150°F
Sound Control (STC) Good — STC 39–45 Excellent — STC 45–52
Moisture Resistance Can absorb moisture Hydrophobic — drains, won't hold water
Density / Rigidity Flexible batts Semi-rigid — holds shape in open cavities
Best Applications Walls, attics, floors, crawl spaces Party walls, commercial, floors, soundproofing
Price Point Lower cost per sq ft Premium — typically 20–30% more
Availability Full R-value range stocked AFB, R-15, R-23, R-30 stocked
