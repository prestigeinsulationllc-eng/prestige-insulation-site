Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Overview

Prestige Insulation LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website prestigeinsulationco.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information you provide when using our site. We are committed to handling your information responsibly and transparently.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide through our contact and quote request forms, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Delivery address
  • Project details and material requirements

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising networks, or third-party analytics tools that collect personal data without your knowledge.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information you submit is used solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry or quote request
  • Coordinate material delivery and logistics
  • Send invoices or order confirmations
  • Follow up on open orders

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties. No mobile phone data or customer information will be shared with third parties.

4. Data Storage & Form Processing

Form submissions on our site are processed through Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form handling service, and forwarded to our business email (Info@PrestigeInsulationco.com). Formspree may store submission data temporarily to deliver the email. Please refer to Formspree's Privacy Policy for details on their data handling practices. We retain communications only as long as necessary to fulfill your order or respond to your inquiry.

5. Cookies

Our website does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. Basic browser session behavior may apply, but no personally identifiable information is stored in cookies on your device.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted on Vercel and served via GitHub. These services may collect standard server logs (IP address, browser type, page requested, timestamp) as part of normal infrastructure operation. These logs are not used by us to identify individuals.

7. Your Rights

You may request that we delete any personal information you have submitted to us. To make such a request, contact us at Info@PrestigeInsulationco.com. We will respond within 10 business days.

8. Children's Privacy

Our site is intended for use by adults and business professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 18.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
